How this works

A creature is a tree of boxes joined by hinges. Every hinge follows one body-wide rhythm, shifted and scaled by a handful of numbers, and can listen to a few senses: touch, which way is up, where the goal is.

Evolution starts from thousands of random bodies. Each is dropped into the habitat and scored. The best of every shape is kept, copied with small random changes, and scored again, a few million times over. Each habitat was bred as six separate families that never mixed.

What you watch is not a recording. The same physics that scored each animal is running here in your browser, and gives the same result to the last digit.
