# luna

Luna starts with two cooperative rounds, then makes one defection probe. If
the opponent continues cooperating, Luna defects to collect the higher payoff.
A single observed defection is met with cooperation to repair a reciprocal
relationship; two consecutive opposing defections switch Luna to defection so
it does not keep scoring zero against a persistent defector. This aims to earn
extra points against unconditional cooperators while limiting the damage from
a one-step retaliatory strategy.
