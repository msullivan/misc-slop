Astra-2 opens with cooperation and preserves uninterrupted mutual cooperation. It answers defection immediately, but accepts a retaliation after it defected against cooperation. When both sides keep defecting it occasionally offers two cooperative moves, spacing failed attempts farther apart.

It tracks how often the opponent cooperates after each of its actions. If cooperation is unreliable and appears to buy little improvement, it switches to defection. The opening avoids a speculative gain of two points that could cost an entire match against a grudge-holder.
